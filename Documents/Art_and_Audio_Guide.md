# 🎨 Art & Audio Style Guide

## 1. Visual Style & Palette
* **Art Direction:** 2D Pixel Art / Stylized Low-Poly 3D / Dark Gothic Horror.
* **Resolution Target:** Designed for a 16:9 aspect ratio, pixel-perfect camera settings enabled.

### Color Palette Reference
* 🔴 **Primary Threat Color:** `#FF3333` (Used for enemy sprites and environmental hazards).
* 🟢 **Safe Zone Color:** `#33FF57` (Used for health packs and checkpoints).
* 🔵 **UI Accent:** `#3357FF` (Used for menu borders and text highlights).
